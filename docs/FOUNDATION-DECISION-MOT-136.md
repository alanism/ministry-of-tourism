### What the first useful state means

Show the actual destination/brief and a visible direct action to add a place, note or photo. Relevant FCC material, when available, is inspiration with its origin intact. It is not an invented booking, visited place or personal memory. A missing source does not disable manual collection.

Use one coherent workspace with optional structured views. Make date/time assignment discoverable through the existing engine. Keep the owner's Muse and publication routes available without presenting three competing primary actions. Changing an entry route or destination cannot discard existing material or silently rewrite its origin.

This is a product contract for MOT-120/128/131 and later validation, not authorization to build a second editor or an app prototype under MOT-136.

## Three materially different approaches

| Approach | Customer | Pragmatic | Growth | Money | Ownership and inclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: human visual trip first | Direct collection makes the owner's desired activity visible; must prove that arrangement is not homework. | Reuse TripSession/capture/composition; settle geometry and content contracts. | Personal/host/shared artifacts may encourage reuse; unmeasured. | Intentional apparel discovery without paid inference as an entry requirement; costs remain real. | Manual control and reversible actions; inclusion must be predictable. |
| B: reviewed own-Muse plan first | Useful to people who prefer delegation; separate-device handoff may require effort. | Reuse public guide and proposal/response contracts; prove the real external path. | Existing Muse users are a plausible entry group, not measured acquisition. | No requirement for MoT-funded inference; no claim that every external Muse is free. | Explicit review/Apply/Undo and real receipts; a file grants no room authority. |
| C: publication first | Useful when the person explicitly wants a keepsake; can impose layout work before planning value. | Reuse shared composition/reader; cannot bypass media or selection defects. | Deliberate sharing may help discovery; no measured conversion. | Strong existing shop/keepsake context; no assumed purchases or willingness to pay. | Greatest risk of generic editorial content or merchandising obscuring the selected trip. |

The strongest everyday alternative is chat/Maps plus familiar notes and screenshots. A must earn adoption by reducing copying/repair while retaining personal control. There are no fabricated scores, votes, designer endorsements or population-preference claims in this comparison.

## Founding hypothesis

If MoT helps travelers and organizing households turn scattered ideas and local material into a personal trip through a useful visual workspace, with their own Muse available when wanted, they will prefer it to disconnected chat, Maps and notes because gathering and deciding feel under their control and the same trustworthy trip stays useful during planning, travel and remembering.

Owner selection makes A the current product bet. The preference and differentiation predictions remain unvalidated until the protocol produces real evidence.
